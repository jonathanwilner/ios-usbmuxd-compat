# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss technical evidence and proposed changes.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or disclose private information
about another participant. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts
that violate these rules. Report concerns privately through the repository's
private reporting channel when available. Maintainers should handle reports
confidentially, explain decisions, and provide a way to request reconsideration.
